I built Trip Scheduler to make organising trips with multiple people easier, without the endless WhatsApp admin.

It gives the group one shared place to:

- gather everyone's availability across several possible dates
- compare and choose transport and accommodation together
- keep links and votes in one place, instead of losing them in WhatsApp or locking them inside platforms such as Airbnb
- view an itinerary that updates as plans are agreed

The result is a living trip plan the whole group can contribute to, from the first date poll through to the final itinerary.
