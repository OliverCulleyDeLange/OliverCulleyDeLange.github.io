Tiles is a real-time, shared-table word game inspired by the speed and tactile chaos of playing with physical letter tiles. Two to eight players build their own connected crosswords simultaneously while seeing everyone else's tiles around the same virtual table.

The board is designed to feel spatial rather than document-like: players sit around a table, their areas are outlined in colour, and the whole surface can be panned, zoomed, or rotated with touch and trackpad gestures. Tiles can be dragged individually or as a selection, and loose letters can be tapped into an emerging word.

Rooms use private shareable links and synchronize through a Cloudflare Durable Object. US and British SCOWL dictionaries provide live word validation, and peeling is held until every word in a player's grid is valid. Native iOS and Android wrappers add nearby offline play over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi for places where internet access is unavailable.

The project is built with **Astro**, **TypeScript**, **Capacitor**, and **Cloudflare Workers**.
