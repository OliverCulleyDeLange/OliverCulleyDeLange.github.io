My partner and I love Bananagrams, but playing on a train or plane is awkward. Online versions exist, but need reliable internet. So I built **Tiles**, an internet-free, real-time version of Bananagrams for two to eight players.

## Web or native?

The [**web version**](https://oliverdelange.co.uk/tiles/) needs internet: create a private lobby and share its link. The **native iOS and Android versions** can also play offline over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi.

They are not in the app stores because I'm too cheap to pay Apple and Google. You can build and install them from the [GitHub source](https://github.com/OliverCulleyDeLange/tiles) using Xcode or Android Studio.

Built with **Astro**, **TypeScript**, **Capacitor** and **Cloudflare Workers**, with live SCOWL dictionary validation.
