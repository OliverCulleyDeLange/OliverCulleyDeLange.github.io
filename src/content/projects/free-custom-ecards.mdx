Free Custom Ecards is a collaborative card maker for groups who want to send something more personal than clip art and a novelty font. Pick artwork, customise a folded card, invite other people to add their messages, then send the finished card by email.

It is currently free while I build the platform. The longer-term idea is to feature work from independent artists and pay them whenever their art is used.

## What it does

- Lets a whole group sign the same card from their own links
- Updates the card as people add messages and emoji
- Presents the finished card as a foldable, rotatable 3D card
- Sends the card to its recipient by email
- Supports custom photos as well as artwork from the gallery

Built with **Next.js**, **Supabase** and **Cloudflare Workers**. The editor stores each message and sticker as a separate object, while live updates let several people work on the card together.
